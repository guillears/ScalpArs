## The question
The operator wants $1M a year. $1M a year is about $2,740 a day.
EXHAUSTION (EXH) earns about +0.85 % per trade in the backtest, about 1,090 signals a year (2024-01 → 2026-11, with the DL 582 filter).
FLATBASE (FB) earns about +1.0–1.3 % per trade, about 245 signals a year.
Today the depth guard caps each trade at what fills within 0.15 % of the best price, about $6–9k on these coins.
How much can each signal really carry, with which execution, before impact eats the edge? And what yearly $ is realistic?

## The plain answer
1. **Today's setup tops out at about $100k a year** (market order + today's 0.15 % depth guard). In the pessimistic case (EXH only +0.10 %/trade) it is about $30k.
2. **The binding limit is the book, not the account.** The median EXH coin holds about $9k within 0.2 %, $129k within 1 %, $750k within 5 % on the side we sell into.
   A $50k market order pays about 0.26 % on entry and about 0.25 % again on the stop. At $100k it pays about 0.45 % each way.
3. **Per signal, single venue (Binance only):**
   - Market order: the edge falls to 75 % at about **$17k (EXH) / $29k (FB)**, to half at **$37k / $67k**, and to zero at **$85k / $139k**. The most $ per signal is about $165 (EXH, at $50k) and $406 (FB, at $50k).
   - Best single-venue execution = **half market, half a resting maker sell at the signal price for 15 minutes**: half edge at **$85k (EXH) / $199k (FB)**; about $360 / $1,040 per signal.
4. **Yearly $ plateau (EXH + FB, base edge, before haircut):**
   - today: $98k · market without the skip guard: $290k · TWAP 5–15 min: $220–250k · resting limit at the signal price: $413k · half market + half limit: **$647k** · market across 4 venues: $572k · half market + half limit across 4 venues: **$1.09M**.
   - These caps were picked on the same data. After the locked 30–50 % in-sample haircut, the best case is **about $550–760k a year**. In the pessimistic EXH case it is **about $450k before the haircut**.
5. **$1M a year from these two sleeves alone is not supported by the evidence.** The best path (multi-venue + passive half) reaches it only before the haircut, only at an account of about $1M, and with a fixed-$ worst drawdown of about −$370k.
   To reach $1M reliably we need more independent signals (more sleeves, more coins), not more size per signal.
6. **What to build first, by $ impact per unit of work:** ① replace the skip guard with "size to the book" (≈ ×3 on today's plateau) · ② a passive maker leg at the signal price (≈ ×2 more) · ③ multi-venue execution (≈ ×1.7 more, the most work and new counterparty risk). TWAP does not help EXH: the signal decays too fast.

## Method
- **Trades.** EXH = the exit-grid L2 cell `B|tp10|S2|24` (TP 10 % maker, SL min(1.5 ATR15, 6 %), 24 h) with the DL 582 `EXH_SSC_OIVOL` filter: 3,368 trades, slip-free net +0.85 %/trade. FB = today's Flatbase rules (`sizing_exh_flatbase.fb_trades`): 779 trades, +1.04 %/trade. The book data exists from 2024, so every capacity number uses **2024-01 → 2026-11: 3,135 EXH + 704 FB trades (2.88 years)**.
- **Order book.** data.binance.vision `bookDepth` (USD-M futures): cumulative notional within ±1/2/3/4/5 % of mid, a snapshot every 30–60 s. The ±0.2 % band exists only from about 2026-01-08; before that it is imputed as 0.118 × the ±1 % depth (the 2026 median ratio). Coverage: entry book for 99 % of EXH and 82 % of FB trades; exit book for 99 % of both.
  - **Check vs the engine:** on 13 live orders (11-15 → 11-17), the engine's own 0.15 % fillable stamp ÷ the bookDepth-interpolated 0.15 % depth on the same minute = **median 1.01** (range 0.77–1.64). The public data reads the same book the bot sees.
- **Flow.** 1m futures klines with quote volume and taker-buy volume (the aggTrades aggregated) for the 16 minutes after each signal. The volume traded at or above a price level is estimated from each 1m bar's range (volume spread evenly over high–low).
- **Impact model (instant market order) = the book walk.** Sweep $N through the depth curve; the average price paid is the slippage. Fit on the 123 live fills: `slip = −0.023 + 0.89 × walk` (λ 95 % CI 0.38–1.33; corr 0.27; the old √(N/Q5) model has corr 0.25). Used: slip = 1.0 × walk (a sweep cannot cost less than the visible book), latency constant 0.
- **Executions, priced per trade at each size** (fees 0.05 taker / 0.02 maker, real funding, entry on the bid side, stop / time exit on the ask side at the exit moment, TP a resting maker):
  - **A market** today; **A0** market with today's guard (0.15 %, thinner side, shrink, skip below 0.25 ×).
  - **B resting limit** sell at signal open × (1 + x %), W = 5 / 15 min. Fills on a 1-tick trade-through on 1m bars; the filled size ≤ 50 % of the volume that traded at or above the limit in the window (sensitivity 25 % / 100 %); misses earn 0; the filled trade is re-simulated from its fill (adverse selection included).
  - **C TWAP** over 1 / 3 / 5 / 15 min, 4 child orders a minute, ≤ 5 / 10 / 20 % of the real 1m volume. Each child pays the signal decay from the real path, its own book walk (the book is assumed to refill between children), plus 0.5 × the price level reached by every earlier child (permanent impact).
  - **D mix** 50 % market + 50 % limit (at +0 % or +0.2 %).
  - **E multi-venue**: the same order split over Binance + Bybit + OKX + Bitget; books scaled by the measured ×2.0, traded volume by ×1.36, +0.005 % blended taker fee.
- **Yearly frontier.** Account A → notional per trade min(0.183 × A for EXH / 0.305 × A for FB — the 11-18 sizing report's recommended fractions, the per-sleeve $ cap). Yearly $ = Σ(notional × net %) ÷ 2.88 years, no compounding. The cap is the flat $ size that maximised the sleeve's yearly $ (chosen in-sample → haircut).
- **Delay.** Each EXH trade re-simulated with the live exit from an entry 0–15 min late (1m path; parity with the exit grid: 99.9 % of trades within 0.01 %). FB on 5m bars (0 / 5 / 10 / 15 min; 99.6 % within 0.15 %).

How to read it:
- The account reaches the plateau at about $160k (today), $500k (single-venue mix) and about $1M (4-venue mix). Beyond that more money adds nothing.
- The yearly $ is lumpy: EXH's 25-H1 and 26-H1 are weak, and FB lost in 25-H2 at large size. One third of months lose money at every plateau.

## Recommendation (in order of $ impact per unit of engineering)
1. **Replace "skip when it does not fit" with "size to the book" (single venue, market).** Plateau $98k → about $290k (base). Engineering: the guard already reads the book. Change it from a 0.15 % fit test to a cost cap: size each order so the modelled walk on both sides stays ≤ about 0.15–0.25 % (≈ EXH $17–37k, FB $29–67k at the median book), never skip just because the planned size is larger. One config + engine change, with a live-slippage revert gate. **First, measure live slippage at $10–50k:** no live fill has exceeded about $16k, so this region is model, not evidence.
2. **Add a passive maker leg at the signal price.** Post half the size as a post-only SELL at the signal price (never higher) for 15 minutes, cancel the rest; take the other half at market. Plateau about $650k (range $550–720k with the fill-share assumption). Engineering: an order manager for post-only orders, partial fills, cancel-on-timeout, and exits (TP / SL / time) sized to what actually filled. The capped-entry limit (DL 553) is a start.
3. **Multi-venue execution (Bybit, OKX, Bitget).** About ×1.7 on top of item 2 (to about $1.09M before the haircut). Engineering is large: per-venue clients, symbol and contract-size mapping, one position and risk view across venues, per-venue margin, funding, liquidation monitoring, latency to each venue. It needs new exchange accounts and new counterparty risk: the operator's decision, not ours.
4. **Do not build TWAP for EXH.** The signal loses 0.24 % in 5 minutes; that costs more than it saves. A 5–15 min TWAP is acceptable for FB only, and the passive leg beats it.
5. **Stops stay market orders.** At the plateau sizes the stop walk is 0.4–0.8 % on 1 stop in 10 (p90). Price it into every size decision; do not replace stops with limits (a missed stop in a squeeze is the ruin case).
6. **What else scales $ without diluting the edge:** more independent signals. EXH + FB give about 1,330 signals a year. Even at the 4-venue mix's $575 / $1,544 per signal, $1M needs about 1,700–2,000 such signals a year. That means more sleeves, or more coins (coins listed only on Bybit / OKX / Bitget could run the same signal on their own data — untested). The UNIFIED studies are the place for that. Lower fee tiers at high volume also help a little (taker 0.05 % is 6 % of the EXH edge per side); not quantified here.

## Assumptions and blind spots
- **Backtest edge, not live.** EXH +0.85 % is in-sample (DL 582 filter and the L2 exit were chosen on these trades); its clean holdout earned +0.10. Every $ above carries that risk; the pessimistic column shows it.
- **Impact above $20k is not validated by live fills.** The book walk assumes the visible book is all there is (hidden liquidity would help; quotes pulling when hit would hurt).
- **bookDepth** is a 30–60 s snapshot of bands around mid. The ±0.2 % band exists only from 2026-01; before that it is imputed (0.118 × the 1 % band). The interpolation between bands is linear.
- **Limit fills** use 1m bars with volume spread evenly over each bar's range, and take 50 % of it (25 % / 100 % shown). Queue position and our own order's effect on the price are not modelled. aggTrades were not used directly; 1m klines carry the same aggregated volume and taker-buy share.
- **TWAP** assumes the book refills between child orders and 50 % permanent impact. EXH decay uses each trade's own 1m path; FB uses the sleeve-average 5m decay.
- **TP size check:** a resting TP fills only as much as trades through it. EXH's TP 5m bar trades $1.2M at the median but $218k at p10. At $100–150k caps, about 1 TP in 10 may fill only partly (FB: $484k median, $79k p10). Not priced.
- **Caps and the frontier are in-sample** and use fixed $ (no compounding), no slot limit beyond one position per pair (EXH) and 4 slots (FB), and funding as recorded at any size.
- **Other venues:** one quiet-time snapshot; no history, no lead / lag, no funding, fees from published schedules.
- **Not tested:** splitting one order across venues in real time, latency, partial-fill exit handling, liquidation risk at plateau sizes (5× leverage on a $150–300k short in a squeezing coin).
