# DIST early trail + arm-state switch — 2026-11-26 (DECISION_LOG 645) — research only, NO CHANGE

**Question (operator):** "almost all distribution shorts have been positive before ending really bad, some even reach +8 % … why don't we
evaluate a trailing, starting at 1 %, based on ATR, similar to momentum longs trailing" → then, for the least-bad cell (start +2 % /
0.5 × ATR): "nothing separates winners vs losers?"

**Short answer:** no. Each of the 16 new trail cells (start +1 / +1.5 / +2 %) loses to today's Exit B in both data periods and in all
7 half-years (backtest, ≈ 1,550 DIST fills). It shrinks drawdown, but the tight cells (0.5–1 × ATR, fixed %) cut the HOLDOUT daily
compound from +0.85 to about +0.2 %/day, and even the 2 × ATR cells only reach ≈ +0.65: less return for less risk, never better. At the
+2 % minute, 0 of 938 coin / order-flow / BTC state tests beat the luck bar, and no all-period tercile of any variable turns the trail
positive. Keep Exit B (for the trail family; other exit families: DIST_EXIT 11-18).
