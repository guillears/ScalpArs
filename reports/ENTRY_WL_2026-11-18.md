# ENTRY_WL: can the ENTRY tell a runner from a give-back? EXHAUSTION_SHORT and DISTRIBUTION_SHORT (2026-11-18, BACKTEST)

Research only. No engine, config or test change. No engine / config change (research only; working data in the *_data folders stays local).
Pre-registered in `ENTRY_WL_2026-11-18_PREREG.md` (frozen 23:05 UTC, before any entry result). Forward lists frozen 23:08 UTC
(`ENTRY_WL_2026-11-18_data/{exh,dist}_frozen.json`) before HOLDOUT was read. HOLDOUT was read once.

## Plain answer
- **EXHAUSTION_SHORT: no.** Nothing in 97 entry-time variables separates runners from give-backs.
  - No test beats the family-wise null (best |z| 3.86 vs a bar of 4.85).
  - The best theory read (thin coins give back more) held in all 4 DESIGN half-years and in the clean 2023-H1 block, then **flipped on HOLDOUT**.
  - A 50-variable theory model scores AUC 0.49 on HOLDOUT (a coin flip).
  - Verdict: **nothing.** Today's EXH entry and exit stay as they are.
- **DISTRIBUTION_SHORT: weakly, not enough to act.**
  - No test beats the null either. The best was 4.55 vs a bar of 4.58, a 2D cell with N 40.
  - One theory read kept its direction on HOLDOUT: **thin coins** (24 h volume ≤ $42M) give back more. That held in 6 of 7 half-years. But the HOLDOUT effect is small (AUC 0.52, Δ +0.38 %/trade, CI includes 0).
  - The thin side still makes money (+0.50 %/trade), so a filter fails the expectancy bar and lowers the daily compound.
  - The 43-variable theory model reached AUC 0.54 on HOLDOUT (CI 0.49–0.60). That is below the 0.55 bar, and its own DESIGN cross-validation was 0.43.
  - Verdict: **OBSERVE** (the thin-coin size tilt, and the theory-model score). It misses the null, the AUC ≥ 0.55 bar, the HOLDOUT CI and the expectancy bar.
- **Why it fails, in one line:** a give-back and a runner start from the same signal candle. The things that differ (whether supply keeps coming for hours) are not visible at entry in any column we have. Thin coins are the one weak, theory-backed exception.

## 1. EXHAUSTION_SHORT

### 1.1 Question
57 % of EXH trades stop out, and 44 % of all trades are give-backs (up +2–4 %, then stopped). The exit cannot tell them from the +10 % runners. Can the entry?

### 1.2 Theory (pre-registered)
- **Runners need fuel:**
  - a pump still near its high;
  - deep volume decay;
  - trapped longs (high L/S, positive funding, OI built into the top);
  - a falling market;
  - a deep, liquid coin;
  - an early signal or a failed re-test of the high.
- **Give-backs come from:**
  - crowded shorts (negative funding, low L/S);
  - a pump already spent;
  - a thin coin that is easy to squeeze;
  - a rising market.
