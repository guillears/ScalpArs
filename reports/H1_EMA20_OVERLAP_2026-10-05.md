# Is "BTC 1h EMA20 falling" a new momentum-LONG signal? Overlap with the live 1h-slope gates (2026-10-05)

Read-only research. Nothing in services/, config, templates or tests was touched. Pipeline: `scripts/h1_ema20_overlap.py` (inputs: the FILTER_REGIME_MATRIX run's de-duplicated, replica-priced yr5 admitted ML fills `calib.csv`; per-seed engine stamps from `reports/ENGINE_REPLAY_YR5_ML_fills.csv`; `reports/MASTER_POOL_stacked.csv`). Raw tables: `reports/H1_EMA20_OVERLAP_2026-10-05_tables.md`, per-fill data `reports/H1_EMA20_OVERLAP_2026-10-05_fills.csv`.

## Plain-language answer

**This is not a new signal. It is the engine's own BTC 1h slope, seen from a slightly different angle.**

- **The tag and the live gate input agree on direction for 97.5 % of fills.** The tag is "the 1h EMA20 fell over the last closed hour". The gate input, `entry_btc_1h_slope`, is the 3-bar EMA20 % change including the forming hour.
  - 475 of the 492 "falling" fills have an engine slope of −0.05 or below.
  - That is exactly the negative flank that LONG_BTC1H_DEADBAND deliberately lets through. The gate blocks only (−0.05, +0.025).
  - So "skip momentum longs while the 1h EMA20 is falling" really means "close the dead-band's negative flank too" (block every long with engine 1h slope < +0.025). That would **extend an existing gate** and reverse its Jul-5 / Jul-20 founding evidence ("the pullback flank wins"). It would also switch off the 1hPullback_L 2× cell, which lives inside that flank.
- **The loss is not concentrated near the existing thresholds. It is spread across the whole negative flank.**
  - 47 % of the falling cohort's net loss comes from slope ≤ −0.20 and 43 % from (−0.20, −0.10]. Only 10 % comes from the strip just outside the dead-band, (−0.10, −0.05].
  - The engine-slope deciles show a step, not a dose-response. Every decile at or below ≈ −0.07 is negative (−0.11 … −0.19 %). Every decile above is flat (−0.06 … +0.09 %).
  - Nudging the dead-band edge would not capture it.
- **On yr5 the gap looks real but fragile in day units.**
  - Pooled: falling −0.125 % vs rising −0.001 %, gap −0.124 [−0.229, −0.014].
  - Robust across subsets: the sign holds in both halves, in leave-one-month-out (gap −0.105 … −0.162), and in all 3 seeds.
  - Shuffled-day null: two-sided p = 0.027.
  - Fragile in day units:
    - Mean of per-day means for falling days: −0.086 [−0.173, +0.001]. The CI touches 0.
    - Each half's gap CI includes 0.
    - **On the 75 days that had both states, falling and rising hours did equally well (paired gap +0.001 [−0.151, +0.146]).** The effect is "some days are bad and those days are mostly falling-tagged", not "falling hours are bad".
  - The lead was also the most striking of 21 splits in the matrix report's admitted-fills table, so p = 0.027 does not survive that selection.
- **Real fills refute the unconditional rule.**
  - Master kept ML fills in the falling state: 44 fills · 27 d · 77 % WR · **+0.154 %**, above the sleeve's breakeven WR of 63.9 %.
  - Skipping them would have cost **−6.8 %-points** and removed 34 winners.
  - Without the washed-out Jun-18→Jul-2 window the direction agrees: falling −0.040 % (31 fills, 18 d) vs rising +0.174 % (70 fills). But N is small and the CI is wide.
  - The washed-out window was falling-heavy: 13 of 13 falling fills won, avg +0.615 %. That is exactly the regime the pullback flank was kept for.
- **Recommendation: do not arm anything.** At most, pre-register an observe-only candidate defined on the engine's own stamp (below). Separately, flag the **1hPullback_L 2× cell for a cell-registry review**: on yr5 it is 94 fills · 68 % WR · −0.112 % at 1× [−0.245, +0.019], a 2× multiplier sitting on a negative replay cohort.

## 7. Pre-registered observe-only candidate (frozen; NOT armed, no code change needed)

- **Name:** ML_B1H_NEGFLANK.
- **Definition (frozen):**
  - Who: momentum-LONG fills, full-size, non-probe, non-MANUAL, closed.
  - Condition: the stamped `entry_btc_1h_slope` ≤ −0.05, i.e. admitted below the dead-band.
  - Why the stamp: it is already an Order column (rides the CSV) and agrees with H1_EMA20 falling on 97.5 % of fills.
  - Reported alongside: H1_EMA20_UP at `opened_at` (closed bar) as a secondary tag.
  - The 1hPullback_L 2× sub-cell is read separately, and always at 1×.
- **Cohort floor:** fills opened ≥ 2026-10-06 00:00 UTC.
- **Bar (the expectancy filter bar), all required:**
  - ① WR below the sleeve's breakeven WR, computed on the same-period kept ML fills.
  - ② Avg % < 0 at 1×, with 95 % confidence by a **day-clustered** bootstrap.
  - ③ At least 8 distinct days, and no single day or pair carrying ≥ 50 % of the cohort loss.
  - ④ N ≥ 15.
  - ⑤ Additionally, the paired within-day read (falling vs rising on days with both) must not contradict it.
  - ⑥ Results are shown with and without any washed-out-type window (BTC ≥ 15 % below its 30-day high).
- **If it passes:** the next step is a cell verdict on 1hPullback_L (caps/sizing before filters), then a dead-band re-scope proposal with cohort completeness, a 30–50 % haircut and a tight revert gate. It never becomes a direct arm.
- **If it fails:** close it, and keep the negative flank open (the Jul-5/Jul-20 reading stands).

## 8. Blind spots (what this could NOT test)

- **yr5 is a replay, and the lead was found on it.**
  - Every yr5 number is in-sample for this lead. It was selected as the most striking of 21 splits, and the p = 0.027 null does not correct for that.
  - The replica's absolute level is also offset: the sleeve is −0.054 % on yr5 vs about +0.19 % on master. Only the gap is meaningful.
- **Entry convention.** Replica entry is opened_at + 60 s, taker, BASE exit stack.
  - As-replayed pricing (the engine's own exits) gives the same gap (−0.104), so the conclusion does not depend on the replica.
- **Capacity is not modelled.** Skipping 44 % of fills frees slots that other entries could take. The Δ is a no-substitution estimate.
- **The 1hPullback cell's live 2× sizing is ignored.** All numbers are 1×, so the rule's live $ effect would differ.
  - In the master zone table the cell zone is not exactly the cell, because ADX is not applied there.
- **Master is thin and regime-skewed.**
  - The falling state covers 27 days, and 13 of its 44 fills sit in the washed-out window.
  - Master only covers Jun-18 → Oct-2, so it cannot test Jan–Apr.
  - It also contains only fills the live stack kept at the time (era-specific filters).
- **The CALM3D door can never be in the falling state** (its b1h > 0 leg). The falling cohort is therefore almost entirely UNMATCHED (486 of 492), and CALM3D cannot be compared across states.
- **The tag is computed from closed bars, while the engine includes the forming bar.**
  - 17 falling-tag fills had an engine slope ≥ +0.025, and 11 rising-tag fills had an engine slope ≤ −0.05.
  - These disagreements are too few to test whether the lag itself carries information.
- **Other variables were not swept.** No interaction with BTC 5m ADX, breadth or pair variables was screened here. The day-level effect (bad days) may be another daily regime variable that correlates with the 1h EMA20, which is the uniform-degradation caution.
