# WILLY HT under TP +6 % / SL −4 % — what separates winners from losers? (2026-11-18)

Research only. No code, config or engine change. Scripts: `scratchpad/ht6/{pathfacts,prep,analyze,eval}.py`.
Files: `reports/HT_TP6_WL_2026-11-18_trades.csv` (every rebuild HT trade, labels + features + path group),
`_separators.csv` (top screen rules per label), `_candidates.csv` (candidate deep reads), `_4h_trend.csv` (4 h trend at every granularity),
`_event_study.png` (price paths).

## Question

The operator asked: with WILLY HT on TP +6 % / SL −4 %, does anything separate winners from losers? Is it the 4 h trend? Anything else?

## Plain answer

- **The 4 h trend does not separate.** We tested the coin's 4 h EMA20 slope, its 4 h EMA50 slope and BTC's 4 h slope. We cut each by sign, median, terciles and quintiles. The direction flips between half-years. The pattern is not monotonic. Neither side passes the expectancy bar (P(avg ≥ 0) = 0.20–0.25). On real fills it points the other way from the rebuild.
- **Nothing passes as a filter.** One family of rules shows up the same way in all four half-years of the rebuild: **crowded longs** (top-trader or global account long/short above the median), **the perp premium falling in the last hour**, and **entries within 1.4 % of the spike high**. But:
  1. The whole-screen null does not clear it. 244 1D + 1,980 2D rules on booked P&L give p = 0.083 at 90 m and p = 0.37 at 24 h. Each single rule's family-wise p is 0.25–0.99.
  2. **Real fills disagree on the strongest rules.** The crowded-long rules block 9 of the 15 kept master fills, and those 9 were mostly winners (9 · 89 % · +5.07). Master would drop from +3.42 to +0.95 per trade.
  3. Even after the best block, the rebuild's Kelly at the 95 % floor stays ≤ 0 (−0.013 at best). Filtering does not create a proven edge.
- **The bigger problem is not a separator. It is the exit and the trade set.**
  - On the rebuild, 76 % of HT trades go +1 % before −4 %. Only 36 % reach +6 % before −4 % (29 % inside 90 m). Median reach before the stop is +3.6 %.
  - The largest loss bucket is GAVE_BACK: 155 trades, −502 pts. These went +1 % or more, then hit −4 %. That is larger than the entry-side losses (−397 pts). Fix the exit first.
  - The real bot's fills behave very differently: 93 % WORKED and 73 % reach +6. Matched trades: 13 · 62 % · +2.26 under TP6. In-window rebuild trades with no real fill: 22 · 32 % · −0.66.
  - Until the rebuild reproduces the real trade set, the rebuild cannot judge TP6/SL4 HT, and neither can a separator built on it.

**Verdict: NO filter. 4 h trend: NO. Crowded-long family: OBSERVE only** (frozen thresholds below). The exit question stays with WILLY_EXIT_V2 and the backtest audit.

## Method

- **Trades.** 417 rebuild WILLY HT trades (2025-02-03 → 2026-11-17). Each is priced on 1m bars under `F1 TP6/SL4/90m`, which is the master's hold, and also under `/240m` and `/1440m`. Slippage is 0.10 %, with real funding. The exit is parity-checked: "hit +6 before −4" from my own path pass agrees 100 % with the simulator's TP reason at 24 h.
- **Holdout D.** 2023-H2, 201 trades. **This is its third read**, so it is no longer a clean holdout. It is reported, not trusted.
- **Master.** 15 real kept HT fills (B25 8, B26 6, B27 1), re-priced on 1m. The 11-18 paper EVAA fill booked +2.00 at 3 min under today's exit. Under TP6 it cannot be priced yet, and its features are not scored.
- **Labels.**
  - Booked P&L at 90 m and 24 h.
  - Entry label WORKED: +1 % gross before the −4 % net stop, within 24 h, with stop-before-TP inside a bar.
  - REACH: the best gross move before the stop (24 h). Also whether it reached the +6 % TP price before the stop.
- **Path groups.** The `path_labels.group` rules: NEVER_WORKED / STOPPED_RECOVERED (+1 % within 2 h after the stop) / GAVE_BACK / DRIFT_LOSS / LEFT_ON_TABLE / CAPTURED.
- **Screen.**
  - 1D: 46 variables at median, lower and upper tercile, sign (signed variables) and theory cut, both sides → 244 block rules.
  - 2D: every pair of variables at each one's best cut, OR and AND → 1,980 rules.
  - A rule counts only if it blocks ≥ 15 trades, keeps ≥ 30, and blocks at most 50 % (a filter, not a new sleeve).
  - Score: the number of half-years (25-H1, 25-H2, 26-H1, 26-H2) where blocked avg < kept avg, then kept-minus-all avg.
  - Null: 300 within-half label shuffles, re-running the whole 1D+2D screen each time. The statistic is the best kept-minus-all among rules consistent in all 4 halves.
  - Run separately for booked 90 m, booked 24 h, WORKED and REACH.
- **Candidates.** Each is checked for:
  - kept vs blocked per half-year, D, master, matched and unmatched;
  - family-wise p;
  - the expectancy bar on the blocked cohort (WR < sleeve breakeven 42 %, day-clustered bootstrap, ≥ 8 days, no day or pair ≥ 50 % of the loss, N ≥ 15);
  - Kelly at the one-sided 95 % Wilson floor, with n = distinct days;
  - DCR at X = 1, per half and per master batch (calendar days: B25 10-23→11-11, B26 11-11→11-14, B27 11-15→11-17);
  - leave-one-out;
  - a joint check across today's exit, 90 m, 4 h, 24 h, WORKED, REACH and reach +6.
- **Window units.** None of the top candidates is market-wide. All are coin-level. Distinct days are still shown (107–178 per blocked cohort; biggest day ≤ 4 % of the loss).

## Assumptions and caveats

- The rebuild ≠ the real bot. In the real window (10-23 → 11-17) it has 35 HT trades. Only 13 match a real fill. The real bot also has 3 kept fills the rebuild lacks (JCT 11-08, AKE 11-07, IDOL 11-13, all +6).
- The audit (`WILLY_BACKTEST_AUDIT_2026-11-18_trades.csv`, no write-up yet) labels the unmatched HT trades since 10-09. Under TP6/90m:

  | Cause | N | Avg/trade | Note |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | HT not live yet | 12 | +0.24 | |
  | Alpha coins excluded | 7 | −2.65 | Live has read Alpha coins since 11-04, so these are **real for today's bot** |
  | Re-anchored episode | 6 | +0.29 | Rebuild artefact |
  | Depth guard | 2 | −4.07 | |
  | Restart | 1 | −4.10 | |
  | Displacement | 2 | +6.00 | |
  | Other | 4 | mixed | |

  No full-history guard from the audit exists yet. Every read here is on all rebuild trades, plus the matched subset (13) and the in-window unmatched (22).
- **Feature parity is imperfect.** `off_peak` differs between rebuild and master for the same trade by up to ~1 pt (UAI −0.97 vs −1.92, INIT −0.45 vs −1.19). Around the −1.4 cut, the side flips. The other top variables match.
- `spot_share` exists only for coins with a spot market (≈ 55 %). Unscored = kept.
- Δ figures are in-sample. Apply a 30–50 % haircut.

## Blind spots

- Not scored at all:
  - Alpha-listing flag (the audit shows Alpha HT trades lose: 7 · 14 % · −2.65);
  - order-book depth on both sides;
  - liquidation flow;
  - the episode re-anchor flag;
  - the coin's prior Frenzy count;
  - real-fill slippage.
- `s4h_ema50` is missing on master.
- The 11-18 EVAA paper fill is unscored.
- Market-wide variables (BTC slopes, breadth, mkt_frenzy_24h) never made the top lists, so no day-unit read was needed beyond the distinct-day counts.

## Verdict

- **NO filter.** No candidate clears all three tests: the whole-screen null, the master check and a positive Kelly floor after the block.
  - Crowded-long rules (C1, C2) fail the master check: B25 DCR +1.91 → +0.39/+0.95, B26 +3.77 → +0.41.
  - C3, C4, C5 and C6 fail family-wise p (0.08–0.99), and/or holdout D, and/or the LOO check.
- **4 h trend: NO** at every granularity (tables below).
- **OBSERVE, frozen thresholds** (pre-registered, never re-fit):
  - `top_acc > 1.19` OR `glob_acc > 1.0624` (crowded longs);
  - `off_peak > −1.4` measured on the real fill.
  - Track both on every real HT fill under TP6/SL4.
  - Read at ≥ 30 real fills / ≥ 10 days. Promote only if the blocked cohort passes the expectancy bar on real fills.
- **Next step.**
  1. The audit should finish and add its guards: the re-anchor exclusion, and HT live-date / restart handling.
  2. Re-run `ht6/eval.py` on the guarded rebuild.
  3. Add an Alpha-listing flag to the HT feature set. On the audit window Alpha HT trades are 7 · −2.65, and live now trades them. It is the most concrete live-relevant lead, but N = 7.
  4. The TP6/SL4 exit itself is −0.24/trade on the full rebuild (Kelly floor −0.13) against +3.42 on 15 master fills. That gap is the decision to resolve, not a filter.
