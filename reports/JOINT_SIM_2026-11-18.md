## Plain answer
1. **At today's size (0.609 × equity per trade on every sleeve), the four short sleeves together are at about full Kelly.** Under live-like slippage or the in-sample haircut, they are above it.
   - In-sample: **+1.66 %/day**, max drawdown **−65 %**, worst day **−42 %** (2026-01-24, 17 stops).
   - A −50 % drawdown happens in **99.9 %** of the bootstrap paths over the 3.4 years.
2. **The expected rate depends mostly on how much of the in-sample edge is real, and on slippage.**

   | case | %/day |
   |---|---|
   | in-sample | +1.66 |
   | 50 % haircut on L4 + DIST | +1.26 |
   | +0.10 % slip per side | +1.17 |
   | +0.20 % slip per side | +0.68 |
   | 50 % haircut and +0.10 % slip | +0.77 |

   Max drawdown is −65 % to −82 % in every one of these cases.
3. **DISTRIBUTION adds the most.** It adds +0.15 to +0.47 %/day over EXH + FB in every case.
4. **L4 (EXHAUSTION WIDE) adds about nothing once it is haircut.**
   - In-sample it adds +0.08 %/day.
   - With the 50 % haircut it adds −0.005 %/day.
   - With +0.20 % slip it adds −0.08 %/day.
   - It also deepens the drawdown.
5. **WILLY / HT make the book worse in every case.** They cost −0.10 to −0.24 %/day and deepen the max drawdown by 7 to 14 points.
6. **Book depth is the binding limit, not the edge.**
   - Under the SIZING depth rule (skip when the plan is more than 2 × the book), trading stops at about $23k of equity, because 0.609 × equity is then above $14k.
   - The account then flat-lines. Every start ($1k or $10k) ends near $30k.
   - Under today's engine rule (DL 583, shrink to ¼ before skipping), it flat-lines near $57k.
   - Only if trades shrink to the book and are never skipped does the account keep growing: $10k → $340k over 3.4 years, about +0.29 %/day.
7. **Sized by edge** (SIZING's 0.183 for EXH / L4 / DIST and 0.305 for FB), the same four sleeves give **+0.66 %/day** in-sample and +0.54 %/day with the haircut.
   - Max drawdown is −31 % / −33 %.
   - The worst day is −17 %.
   - P(DD ≤ −50 %) is 2.5 % / 8.5 %.

  - EXH, L4 and DIST use the live EXHAUSTION exit: TP min(20 × ATR, 10 %) as a maker order, SL min(1.5 × ATR, 6 %), 24 h.
  - FB: 10 % stop, 5 % TP, 72 h.

**Joint account rules:**
- **Size:** each trade is 0.609 × equity at entry. Equity is realised and marked on close, so it compounds per trade.
- **One position per coin across all sleeves.**
  - A signal on a coin that is already open is skipped.
  - When signals tie at the same time, the order is EXH > L4 > FB > DIST > WILLY.
  - So a WIDE episode on a coin that has a FRENZY episode is the same position.
- **Book:** at most 10 open positions (`max_open_positions_hard`), and a full book skips the signal.
- **Free margin (the engine's `investment ≤ tradeable`):** margin = notional ÷ 5 must fit in 0.975 × equity minus the open margin.
  - If it does not fit, the trade shrinks to what fits, or is skipped if less than ¼ fits.
  - At 0.609 this binds at about 8 open positions, before the book of 10. Unrealised P&L is ignored.
- **Capacity:**
  - The SIZING depth model: fillable ≈ 3,505 × q5^0.054 ≈ $7k.
  - If the plan fits, the trade is full size. If at least ½ fits, it shrinks to the fill. Otherwise it is skipped.
  - WILLY has no q5 column, so it uses the median EXH book.
  - Results are shown with and without the cap. The main tables are uncapped, which is the edge view.
- **Costs and stresses:** the stress cases add 0.10 / 0.20 % per taker leg (entry plus a taker exit). Haircuts subtract 30 % or 50 % of the sleeve's mean from every L4 and DIST trade.
- **DCR:**
  - %/day is the compound rate over every calendar day, from the per-trade compounding path.
  - The one-size DCR (DL 503: fills × 0.609, compounded daily) is shown beside it. The two agree within 0.03.
- **Bootstrap:** 2,000 paths of 10-day blocks over 1,236 days for the drawdown odds. 5,000 paths of 10-day blocks for the balance paths.

## Assumptions and limits
- **Backtest trades, not fills.** EXH has 1 paper fill and FB has 20 – 28 master fills. L4 and DIST have none.
- **In-sample:**
  - DIST's RSI1h cut and L4's cell were picked on these years, hence the 30 – 50 % haircut.
  - EXH's live exit was also picked after the fact, and it gets no haircut here.
  - EXHAUSTION was ≈ 0 in 2020 – 23-04 (LEADS_DEEP).
- **Costs:**
  - A flat 0.10 % per taker leg, a maker TP, and real funding (including hourly squeeze coins where they happened).
  - Live median slippage is ≈ 0.02 %, with thin-coin outliers of 0.3 – 0.6 %. That is why the +0.10 / +0.20 stresses matter.
- **Coin lock on fixed trade lists:**
  - Each sleeve's list was already one position per pair inside that sleeve.
  - When the joint lock skips a trade, a later signal on the same coin that the sleeve's own lock had suppressed is not revived. That is a small second-order effect.
- **Engine parity:**
  - Equal-split base (÷ 4) and the reserve schedule are folded into 0.609. Above $25k the schedule lowers the ratio, which the cap overrides anyway.
  - The free margin ignores unrealised P&L (cross margin would count it).
  - FRENZY_LONG and the momentum sleeves taking slots or margin are not modelled.
  - Trades open and close at their backtest times. There are no restart gaps (live restarts skip spikes).
- **Depth model:**
  - A 10-stamp fit (11-15 → 11-18).
  - WILLY uses the median EXH book.
  - DL 584's impact mode is not modelled beyond the ½ / ¼ shrink rules.
- **Bootstrap:**
  - 10-day blocks.
  - The DD odds describe a 3.4-year run.
  - The 30 / 60 / 90-day bands reuse history's day mix, including the strong 2025-H2 → 2026-H2 era. A weaker regime (like 2020 – 21) is not in the sample.
