PATH / PRICING RULES (1m)
  Net % = the bot's paper net (WT.net_pct: gross - 0.045 entry taker - 0.045 x exit/entry). Bars from the first full minute after te
  (entry minute skipped, house convention). Resting maker TP: fills on a bar whose high >= the RM threshold (limit + 0.5 tick through),
  books the maker net. Stop: bar low <= line -> min(line, bar open) - 0.2. Bar touching both TP and stop = STOP first. Cap: close of the
  last bar <= te + 720 min, -0.2. Path ending before resolution = UNRES (excluded, listed).
  TOUCH EVENT: first 1m bar (k) whose high >= +1.00 % net (= entry x (1 + 1 % + fees)) while C is still open. If bar k also hits C's
  stop or C's TP, C exits in bar k and every at-close policy has Δ = 0 (cannot act). Decisions act at the CLOSE of bar k (features use
  only bars closed by then; 5m data = last 5m bar closed by then; breadth grid = row with t <= close_k - 5 min).

POLICIES (vs C; non-touch fills Δ = 0)
  P1    bank +1 always: a resting maker limit at +1 from entry (= the +1 TP / -3 stop / 12 h exit; touches bar k regardless of C's TP).
  P1c   bank at the touch-bar close (market, -0.2) for every touch fill.
  P2    = P1c only inside a feature zone.
  P3_L  at the touch close move the stop to L in {0 (break-even), +0.3, -1}; C's TP stays; stop-type exit -0.2; all touch fills and
        inside a zone.
  P4_D  at the touch close arm a trail: line = running max high (through the previous bar) - D, D in {0.5, 0.8, 1.0}, floor -3,
        C's TP stays; all touch fills and inside a zone.
  Zoneable policies (screened): P2, P3_L0, P3_L0.3, P3_L-1, P4_D0.5, P4_D0.8, P4_D1.0 (7).
