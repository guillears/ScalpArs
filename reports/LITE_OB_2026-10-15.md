## Plain-English answer

**How many touches.** We have 23 LITE trades that touched +1 % with a book snapshot just before the touch:
- 15 real trades;
- 8 emulated signals the bot did not take.

**How many died.** Under today's C exit, **7 died** at −3 (4 real: MOVR, BAT 10-12 06:45, DUSK, ZK 10-15). **16 went on**: 2 reached +2 but not +3 (EPIC, BTW) and 14 reached +3.

**Was the book different just before the touch?** No. The minute before +1 looks the same for dying trades and runners:
- imbalance within 0.5 / 1 / 2 %;
- ask $ and bid $ within 1 %;
- ask-wall distance and size;
- spread.

All of these have p ≥ 0.2 and family-wise p = 1.00. The same is true at entry (dead-on-arrival vs not): family-wise p 0.77. This matches the price/indicator study. Nothing visible at +1 tells DUSK / ZK apart from EPIC / DEEP.

**What does differ comes 1–2 minutes AFTER the touch.** Two book signals stand out:

1. **Ask depth within 1 % shrinks in the 2nd minute after the touch on the dying trades.**
   - Median change from minute +1 to +2: **−13 %** for dying trades vs **+10 %** for runners.
   - Family-wise p **0.018** over 180 features, after removing snapshots taken at or after the exit minute.
   - The sign is the same on real (z −2.6) and emulated (z −2.5).
   - Real trades:

     | | trades (change in ask depth) |
     |---|---|
     | Dying | MOVR −8 %, BAT −16 %, DUSK −5 %, ZK −22 % |
     | Runners | MET +115 %, BAT +27 %, BAT +5 %, STRK +10 %, EPIC +12 %, DEEP +0.4 %, W 0 % |

   - It is NOT a price proxy: rank correlation with the price change over the same minute is 0.00.
2. **The biggest bid wall (largest bid within 2 %) stays put on the dying trades; on runners it shrinks.**
   - Change vs the entry minute, one minute after the touch: **+3 %** for dying trades vs **−30 %** for runners.
   - Family-wise p 0.08 before the leak fix, **0.145 after it**.
   - On the 8 emulated trades with no real overlap, the separation is perfect (3 dying: +3 % … +200 %; 5 runners: −26 … −58 %).
   - It is partly a price proxy (correlation −0.49 with the price at that minute): runners have moved further, so the old wall falls out of the 2 % window.

**Read this as two candidates for the recorder, not a finding:**
- 23 touches (7 died) over 10 days.
- Fast runners that hit TP inside the touch minute drop out of the "+2 min" test, so that sample is 17 (6 vs 11).
- The real and emulated sets share 14 trades, so "same sign on both" is not independent.
- Even if real, a signal at touch +2 min needs a bank-at-+1-ish exit that the earlier study priced at a loss unless more than ~25 % of the flagged touches die.

**+2 runners vs +3 runners (D1).** G2 has only 2 trades (EPIC, BTW), so no comparison is possible. Their medians sit inside or at the edge of the G3 range on every feature (`group_medians_primary.csv`).

**Book × macro (D2, 16 primary combos):**
- No combo passes: best |z| 2.83, family-wise p **0.18**.
- 21 of the 64 quadrants have fewer than 5 touches, so they are descriptive only. Each quadrant holds 2–9 trades.

**Exhaustive book × macro (D3):**
- 184 book × 41 market variables = 16,564 eligible quadrants. Best |z| 3.51 vs null 95 % 4.36: family-wise p **0.85**.
- This is a ranking, not evidence. The top 15 are all re-cuts of the same bid-wall / ask-depth features against the C zone, BTC EMA gap, the LITE vol multiplier or BTC buyer share.

**Suggested recorder pre-registration** (for DECISION_LOG 302, one frozen threshold each, read only on new fills):
- (a) ask $ within 1 %, minute-over-minute change at touch +2 min, below 0;
- (b) the largest bid wall within 2 %, change vs the entry minute at touch +1 min, ≥ 0.

## 6. What minute resolution (and this dataset) cannot see

- **Seconds.**
  - The snapshot is taken about 20 s into each labelled minute; the +1 print can be up to 60 s from the nearest snapshot.
  - The AT snapshot can fall on either side of the touch, which is why PRE is primary.
  - Books change many times a second around a breakout; we see one frame per minute.
- **Trade side per print.** We only have the 1m taker-buy volume. We cannot see who hit whom at the touch, how large the clips were, or whether the move up was one buyer or many.
- **Queue dynamics.** Order adds and cancels between frames are invisible, as are spoofing / pulled walls and iceberg refills. A "wall stays put" reading cannot tell a real resting order from one re-posted every second.
- **Depth beyond 500 levels.** Band readings are set to missing when the levels do not reach the band (rare inside 2 %).
- **Selection.** Snapshots exist only while a pair is FRENZY-flagged (≤ 12 pairs) and only since 10-03 16:00. 4 of the 26 real trades have no frames at all.

## Addendum — adversarial review (2026-10-16) · supersedes the "not a price proxy" line in §After the touch
An independent review (scratch: scratchpad/obreview, a1–a3.py) found candidate (a) is most likely an artefact, NOT a finding:
- **Mask choice:** unmasked, the same feature ranked #4 (fw p 0.55). The D4 mask (strict `k+off < j`) also dropped BRUSDT, a dying trade still
  open at the P2 snapshot whose ask depth GREW (+0.099). Mask "open at the snapshot" (`k+off ≤ j`): 7 vs 12, z −2.87, feature fw p **0.23**.
- **Price proxy:** the "0.00" used 1m closes k+1 → k+2, ~40 s misaligned with the :20 s snapshots. With the snapshots' own mids: pooled
  Spearman +0.28; **within the dying group +0.94** (deep-review re-derivation: total bid+ask depth vs mid change ρ 0.96 with BR / 1.00 without;
  ask-only 0.43).
- **Not ask-specific:** bid $ within 1 % z −2.31 (p 0.021), bid within 0.5 % z −2.51; total depth within 1 % z −3.32 — general liquidity
  thinning as price slips.
- "Same sign real vs emulated" is mostly the same trades (14 of 22 emulated touches are real fills; emulated BAT 10-10 07:35 = the real BAT path).
- Which feature tops the screen swaps with the masking choice (ask lead ↔ bid wall) → noise at this N.
Decision (DECISION_LOG 302): no recorder; one free long-shot line on TOTAL depth (scripts/scout_lite_ob_thin.py), study touches never count.
