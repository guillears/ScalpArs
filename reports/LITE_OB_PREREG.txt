LITE OB STUDY — PRE-REGISTRATION (frozen 2026-10-15, written BEFORE any book-feature x outcome table was computed)
=====================================================================================================================
Question: do FRENZY_LITE trades that touch +1 % and then die at the -3 stop look different in the ORDER BOOK around the +1 touch
than the ones that go on to the C take-profit (+2, or +3 inside the C zone)? Same question at entry (dead-on-arrival vs not).
Status: hint-finder for the live recorder (DECISION_LOG 302). NOT a ship / arm decision whatever the result.

DATA
- Book: BOOK rows of every ~/Downloads/scalpars_decisions_*.csv, dedup (t, pair) -> reports/lite_ob/book.pkl
  (177,430 rows, 81 pairs, 2026-10-03 16:00 -> 2026-10-15 19:25 UTC). Engine (main.py orderbook loop): the snapshot labelled
  minute M is taken at ~M:20 s (sleep to minute + 20 s, label = floor(now) to the minute), 500-level depth.
- Price: Binance 1m klines with taker-buy volume (reports/lite_ob/k1m), 5m klines for the episode rebuild (reports/lite_ob/k5m).

DEFINITIONS (net % = WT.net_pct on the fill price E, the LITE_AT1 convention; levels = O.rm_levels maker TP thresholds)
- C exit: tp = +3 if entry_frenzy_vol_mult >= 20 and BTC 1h EMA20 slope (3 h) > 0 else +2; resting maker TP; stop -3 (+ -0.20 slip);
  12 h cap. For emulated signals vol_mult / slope come from the rebuilt episode / BTC 1h EMA20 on 5m-derived hourly closes.
- TOUCH minute k = the first 1m bar (open_time k) whose net high >= +1.00 before C exits.
- OUTCOME (primary, among touches): DIE = C exits at the stop after the touch; GO = C exits at its TP. CAP / UNRES excluded.
  Secondary (real trades only, descriptive): the as-traded close (STOP_LOSS vs TP) and the raw peak.
- ENTRY OUTCOME: DOA = C stop without ever touching +1 net; NOT-DOA = touched +1 or TP. CAP / UNRES excluded.
- Real trades that never touch +1 net (e.g. SKL / CAP if so) are shown descriptively anchored at their peak minute, never in the test.

SNAPSHOTS (book minute labels; snapshot taken ~20 s into the labelled minute)
- PRE  = label k-1 (certainly before the touch minute opens)          <- PRIMARY
- AT   = label k   (~20 s into the touch minute; may be before or after the touch print: ambiguous)
- P1, P2, P3 = labels k+1, k+2, k+3
- ENT  = label floor(te) (the entry minute, ~7-12 s after the fill); ENTPRE = label floor(te)-1.
  A snapshot missing for a label = feature missing (no interpolation, no nearest-neighbour).

FEATURES (12 levels per snapshot)
  imb_05, imb_1, imb_2                         (bid$ - ask$)/(bid$ + ask$) within 0.5 / 1 / 2 % of mid
  lg_ask_1, lg_bid_1                           log10 $ within 1 % (ask / bid)
  wall_ask_dist, lg_wall_ask_usd, wall_ask_share   largest ask level within 2 %: distance %, log10 $, share of side $ within 2 %
  wall_bid_dist, lg_wall_bid_usd, wall_bid_share   same for bids
  spread                                       spread %
 + change vs ENT (difference; log-ratio for the lg_ $ features)   -> 12 "dE_" features
 + change vs the previous minute's snapshot                        -> 12 "dP_" features
 => 36 per snapshot. TOUCH family = 36 x {PRE, AT, P1, P2, P3} = 180 features.
 ENTRY family = 12 levels at ENT + 12 changes ENT vs ENTPRE = 24 features (outcome DOA vs NOT-DOA).

TESTS (per family; run separately on REAL, EMULATED, POOLED-dedup (an emulated signal matching a real fill on pair & 5m bar counts once,
the real trade's row kept))
- per feature: median DIE vs GO, Mann-Whitney rank-sum two-sided p (normal approx with tie correction), N per side.
- family-wise: DAY-block permutation null (rows sorted by time, UTC-day blocks of the outcome vector shuffled as blocks and re-assigned
  positionally, LITE_AT1 convention), 2,000 draws; statistic = max |z| over the family; per-feature fw-p = P(null max |z| >= |z_f|).
- 2D: only if a sample has >= 40 touches with >= 10 in each class; otherwise stated as not run.
- Pass for "hint" (to name a feature for the live recorder's pre-registered list): fw-p <= 0.10 on the pooled sample AND same sign of the
  median difference on REAL and on EMULATED. Nothing here can pass as a ship; N is small by construction.

EMULATED SIGNALS (more N)
- Pairs = the 81 BOOK pairs. Episodes rebuilt with services.frenzy.frenzy_walk / normal_hour_usd on the fetched 5m klines (the
  lite_theory episode builder, unchanged logic); signals = study_lite_theory_signals.emulate with TODAY's params (need 12, 2-16.75 h,
  vol < 100, red bar, 24 h vol >= $20M), not bearish (frenzy_bearish_block). Entry te = signal bar close + 12 s, E = open of the first
  1m bar at/after te (+ the taker entry slip convention of the theory study is NOT added: E = print, as in the 1m live path).
- Kept for the touch test: signals with T >= 2026-10-03 16:00, a touch, a resolved C outcome, and a PRE or AT snapshot for that pair.
- Correspondence with real fills: same pair and |te_real - te_emul| <= 5 min.

BLIND SPOTS DECLARED UP FRONT: minute resolution (the book can change many times inside a minute; the touch print and the snapshot are up to
60 s apart), no trade side / aggressor per print beyond the 1m taker-buy volume, no queue / cancel dynamics, no book beyond 500 levels, no
spoofing detection, depth band readings set to missing when the 500 levels do not reach the band.

ADDENDUM D1 (operator clarification, 2026-10-15 ~22:35 UTC, declared BEFORE any feature x outcome table was computed) — DESCRIPTIVE ONLY
- Every touch table also shows THREE groups side by side: G1 = touched +1 then died at the stop (= DIE); G2 = touched +1, reached +2
  (the +2 maker threshold) but NOT +3; G3 = touched +1 and reached +3 (the +3 maker threshold).
- G2 / G3 are read on a separate walk with a resting +3 TP and the -3 stop (independent of C's exit, so outside-zone trades that C closes
  at +2 are still split by whether price went on to +3 before -3). A GO trade whose +3 walk ends at the stop / cap / unresolved = G2.
- Per-group medians (and N) per feature are descriptive. The primary test is unchanged: DIE vs GO (= G2 + G3 pooled), same family /
  permutation null. No G2-vs-G3 test enters the family-wise count.

ADDENDUM D2 (operator: ORDER BOOK x MACRO, 2026-10-15 ~22:40 UTC, declared BEFORE any book x outcome or book x macro table was computed)
Sample: touches with a resolved C outcome (DIE vs GO), POOLED (real + non-matching emulated); REAL and EMUL shown descriptively.
4 book features (median split on the pooled touch sample, low <= median < high):
  B1 imbalance within 1 % at PRE                                  PRE_imb_1
  B2 ask $ within 1 % at PRE vs the entry minute (pair-normalised) PRE_dE_lg_ask_1
  B3 ask-wall "distance x size" = wall_ask_share / wall_ask_dist at PRE (big AND near = high)
  B4 bid-depth change over the next 1-3 min = lg_bid_1 at P3 (else P2, else P1) minus PRE_lg_bid_1
4 macro splits:
  M1 BTC move from the entry minute to the touch-minute open (1m closes) <= 0 / > 0
  M2 BTC 1h EMA20 3-h slope at the touch (hourly closes) <= 0 / > 0
  M3 LITE C zone in / out (vol_mult >= 20 and BTC 1h slope at entry > 0)
  M4 market buyer share = BTC 1m taker-buy quote share over the 5 bars before the touch minute, median split
=> 16 combos (B x M), each a 2 x 2 of 4 quadrants. Per quadrant: N, G1 died / G2 +2 / G3 +3, die-rate.
Statistic per quadrant: two-proportion z of die-rate (quadrant vs the rest of the combo's scored rows). Family = all 64 quadrants of the
16 combos; family-wise p = day-block permutation of the outcome vector (same scheme as the main test, 2,000 draws) of max |z|.
Quadrants with < 5 touches are descriptive only (shown, flagged, still inside the max but called out). N is small: a pass here is at most a
recorder hint, never a filter.

ADDENDUM D4 (POST-HOC validity check, written AFTER the first family-test output was seen — declared as such, not a re-fit)
The first run showed snapshots AT / P1..P3 can fall AT or AFTER the C exit minute (e.g. a TP filled in the touch minute), which leaks the
outcome into the "after-touch" features. Correction: a snapshot at label k+off is used only if k+off < the C exit bar index (strictly
before the exit minute); otherwise that row's feature is set missing. The family tests are re-run on the masked data; the masked result
is the headline, the unmasked one is kept for transparency. Also reported: the top feature on the INDEPENDENT emulated subset (emulated
touches that match no real fill) and its rank correlation with the price path at the same snapshot (net close of the snapshot minute,
bid-wall distance) to show whether it is a price proxy.
