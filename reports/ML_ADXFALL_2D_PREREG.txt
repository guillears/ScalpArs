====================================================================================================
Question (operator): "ADX falling + other variable?" Momentum LONG fills whose coin ADX at entry is below the previous bar's
(stamp: entry_adx < entry_adx_prev; DL 273 closed-bar rebuild: c_adx_delta ≤ 0) are consistently but mildly worse than ADX-rising
fills (known: zone − rest −0.07 / −0.07 / −0.12 by era, pooled p 0.001, reports/ML_REGIME_2026-10-16.md Q3; master zone 77 · −0.01
vs rest +0.05). Is there a SECOND variable X that makes ADX-falling fills clearly losing, or splits them into a losing and a fine
part, consistently in the 3 backtest eras AND master AND fresh? (The ADX × BTC-24h-down 2×2 is known and NOT re-tested as new.)

§0 THEORY (plain English, before tests)
---------------------------------------
The momentum long buys a coin that just showed strength on 5m (EMA stack, RSI 50s–60s, ADX). If ADX is already FALLING at that
moment, the directional move that created the signal is losing force: we are buying late, into a move that is exhausting. A late
entry is hurt most when the move is ALSO (a) already extended in price, (b) losing momentum on other gauges at the same time, or
(c) fighting the higher-timeframe trend / BTC. It should NOT matter for things unrelated to move age / exhaustion.

Predicted to SHARPEN the ADX-falling loss (bad side X named; pre-registered primaries P1–P12):
  P1  RSI already high           entry_rsi > 60.1367            (2025-zone upper tercile)  — exhaustion
  P2  RSI rolling over           d_rsi_dir ≤ 0                   (RSI ≤ previous bar)       — second oscillator decaying
  P3  stretched above EMA5       entry_price_vs_ema5_pct > 0.149 (2025-zone median)         — chasing
  P4  stretched above EMA13      entry_dist_from_ema13_pct > 0.372 (2025-zone median)       — chasing
  P5  EMA5–EMA20 gap narrowing   c_gap5_20_chg ≤ 0               (closed bar)               — trend fan closing
  P6  ADX level high             entry_adx > 21.4623             (2025-zone median)         — decaying from a strong trend
  P7  DI spread shrinking        c_dis_chg ≤ 0                   (closed bar, +DI − −DI)    — directional pressure fading
  P8  volume fading              c_vol_tr3 ≤ 1                   (Σ vol last 3 closed bars ≤ prior 3)
  P9  coin 1h trend down         k_slope1h ≤ 0                   (coin 1h EMA20 slope)       — counter-trend bounce fading
  P10 coin 4h trend down         k_gap4h ≤ 0                     (coin 4h EMA20 vs EMA50)
  P11 BTC short-term down        m_btc_r15 ≤ 0                   (BTC 15-min return)
  P12 move already old           c_run_e20 > 8                   (closed bars above EMA20 > 2025-zone median)
Predicted NOT to matter (controls, read only in the exhaustive screen): hour of day, coin size (mcap / 24h volume / rank / age),
pattern-cell flags, BTC 30-day position.
Ambiguity stated up front: P6 could go either way (low ADX falling = no trend at all / chop). Predicted direction: HIGH is worse.

§2 PRIMARY TESTS (P1–P12, read FIRST)
-------------------------------------
For each Pk: Δin = mean(AF∧X) − mean(AF∧¬X) per era, day-clustered z (study_ml_dt2d_fu_analyse dstats). Also Δrest = AF∧X vs
everything else.
PRIMARY PASS (all): ① Δin < 0 in all 3 eras; ② Stouffer z = Σz/√3 ≤ −2.64 (one-sided Bonferroni 0.05/12); ③ master as-traded Δin
not opposite with ≥ 10 fills each side (refute-only); ④ fresh shown (no gate, N tiny).
"CLEARLY LOSING" cohort bar for AF∧X (expectancy bar, CLAUDE.md): per era WR < sleeve breakeven WR (computed per era on all
fills; ≈ 62 %), avg < 0 at ≥ 95 % day-bootstrap, ≥ 8 days, N ≥ 15, no day/pair ≥ 50 % of the loss.
SELECTIVITY (the operative test because the sleeve itself loses in 2025 / H1): refuse AF∧X → kept avg − refused avg > 0 at
P ≥ 0.90 (day bootstrap) in EVERY era; two-sided (refused winners counted); master and fresh shown.

§3 EXHAUSTIVE SCREEN (after the primaries)
------------------------------------------
Variables: the 106 parent variables (reports/ML_DT2D_screen.csv) minus the zone definers (d_adx_dir, entry_adx_delta) + the
follow-up n_* (RSI/ADX 1h/4h level + dir, price-vs-EMA grid 5m/1h/4h) + the 17 c_* closed-bar rebuilds + s_dis + s_gap520_chg.
Cells per variable: 7 quantile ranges frozen on the 2025 AF zone (≤med, >med, T1, T2, T3, ≤q2, >q1; ≤ 3 distinct values → one cell
per value) + sign cells (≤0 / >0) where 10–90 % of the 2025 zone is ≤ 0. Validity: in and out ≥ 5 % of the zone and ≥ 8 days, every
era. Stat: C = signed min |z| over the 3 eras (same sign), for Δin and Δrest.
Null: 200 day-block shuffles of y within era (rng 20261016), family-wise p = share of shuffles whose max |C| ≥ observed.
Walk-forward both directions: (a) discover 2025 |z| ≥ 1.96 ∧ |Δ| ≥ 0.05 → confirm same sign in H1 AND H2; (b) discover on 2026
(H1 ∪ H2 pooled) same rule → confirm same sign in 2025. Counts vs the same-shuffle null.
SURVIVOR = fw-p ≤ 0.05 (Δin or Δrest) OR walk-forward confirmed count above the null 95th with the cell among the confirmed.
For every survivor and the top 3 cells by |C_in| (labelled even if failing): expectancy bar, selectivity, two-sided refusal Δ,
LOMO (leave-one-month-out Δ sign on the pooled backtest), pair / day concentration, master per batch before/after (% and $),
stack_keep, fresh per trade (LIVE rows marked).

§4 DECISION RULE (frozen)
-------------------------
An "ADX-falling × X" cell is a CANDIDATE only if it passes ① primary or survivor status, ② selectivity P ≥ 0.90 in every era,
③ master not refuting, ④ fresh not opposite (N ≥ 5 in AF∧X). Anything less = descriptive. No arm is recommended from this study
(no-arm-before-review); at most an observe line with a frozen bar; cross-period evidence is refute-only.
