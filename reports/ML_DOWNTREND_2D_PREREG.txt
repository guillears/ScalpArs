STEP 0 — THEORY (plain English)
  A momentum long on a coin whose 1h EMA20 is below its 1h EMA200 is buying a 5m pop inside a larger downtrend.
  Two stories compete:
   (a) COUNTER-TREND BOUNCE (fails): the pop runs straight into overhead supply — the falling 1h EMA20/EMA50/EMA200 and the
       sellers who bought higher. Short-covering lifts it for a few minutes, then the higher-TF trend resumes → our SL.
       Worse when the coin is DEEP below the EMA200, when the 1h gap is still WIDENING (trend accelerating down), when the
       price is still BELOW the 1h EMA200 (resistance overhead), when BTC is also falling (no tide), when the coin's 4h trend
       is down too, and when ADX is falling (no fresh thrust).
   (b) EARLY REVERSAL (works): the coin has already turned — the 1h gap is NARROWING fast, the price has RECLAIMED the 1h
       EMA200 (EMA20 just lags), the 4h trend is turning, BTC is rising, volume is expanding, ADX is rising. Then the 5m signal
       is the first leg of a new trend, not a bounce.
  So the theory predicts the zone is NOT uniformly bad: it should split by (i) depth, (ii) gap direction (narrowing vs widening),
  (iii) price vs EMA200, (iv) BTC direction, (v) coin 4h trend, (vi) ADX direction, (vii) volume expansion.

PRE-REGISTERED THEORY PAIRS (tested FIRST; family of 7 → Bonferroni α 0.05/7 = 0.0071 on the 2025 discovery read;
direction fixed below = the "bad" side inside the zone; cut fixed here, never moved)
  T1 DEPTH      bad = g1h ≤ −3.0 %   (deep: EMA20 ≥ 3 % below EMA200)          good = −3.0 < g1h ≤ 0
  T2 NARROWING  bad = Δg1h over the last 4 h ≤ 0 (gap still widening/flat)       good = Δ4h > 0 (narrowing → reversal)
  T3 RECLAIM    bad = price ≤ 1h EMA200                                         good = price > 1h EMA200
  T4 BTC 1h     bad = entry_btc_1h_slope ≤ 0                                     good = > 0
  T5 COIN 4h    bad = coin 4h EMA20 ≤ EMA50 (rebuilt)                            good = > EMA50
  T6 ADX DIR    bad = entry_adx − entry_adx_prev ≤ 0                             good = > 0
  T7 VOLUME     bad = entry_pair_volume_ratio ≤ its 2025-zone median (label-blind) good = above
  For each: inside-zone split Δ = mean(DT ∧ bad) − mean(DT ∧ good) and the block cell DT ∧ bad vs REST (everything else scored).
  Theory is SUPPORTED only if Δ < 0 in ALL three replay eras (2025 / 2026-H1 / 2026-H2, seeds pooled, day-clustered CI) AND the
  2025 read beats Bonferroni on the day-block-shuffled null AND master and the fresh line cohort point the same way.

EXHAUSTIVE SCREEN (after the theory pairs)
  Variables: every numeric entry_* stamp (replay coverage ≥ 90 % in every era, ≥ 2 distinct values; NON_SIGNAL plumbing,
  pattern flags with < 2 levels excluded), the derived d_* stamps (ADX/RSI/BTC dirs, fan ratio, hour), the rebuilt k_* klines
  features (g1h Δ1h/Δ4h/Δ24h, price vs 1h EMA20/EMA200, coin 1h slope, coin 4h gap/slope, coin ret 4h/24h, off 24h high,
  above 24h low, BTC 4h slope, BTC 1h gap20/200, BTC Δg 4h) and the ML_WL_1015 market-impulse features (btc_r*, breadth,
  dispersion, volumes, rs15/rs60).  Columns failing coverage are LISTED as blind spots, not silently dropped.
  Cuts: label-blind, computed ONCE on the 2025 replay ZONE fills (median → 2 halves; terciles → T1 / T2 / T3 / T1∪T2 / T2∪T3),
  then frozen and applied to every other block.  Cells: X-cell inside the zone.
  Statistics per cell per era: inside-zone Δ_in = mean(DT∧X) − mean(DT∧¬X); block Δ_rest = mean(DT∧X) − mean(not(DT∧X)).
  Day-clustered z for each.  Validity: cell ≥ 5 % of the zone fills and ≥ 8 days on both sides, in every era.
  CONSISTENCY SCORE C = min over the 3 eras of the signed z (same sign in all three, else 0); signed both ways (bad / good cell).
  NULL: 200 day-block shuffles (outcome day-blocks permuted in time order, each era independently, seeds kept together),
  max C over the whole search per shuffle → family-wise p = share of shuffles whose max C ≥ the observed C.  Also report the
  count of cells with C ≥ 1.96 vs the null count distribution.
  WALK-FORWARD: discover on 2025 (cells with 2025 Δ_in p < 0.05, |Δ| ≥ 0.05 %), confirm on 2026-H1 and 2026-H2 (same sign, each),
  then master (as-traded and stack_keep views) and the fresh line cohort (same sign = "points the same way").
  Market-wide X (BTC / breadth / regime / ETH / global volume) read in DAY units (Δ on day means) — window-units rule.
