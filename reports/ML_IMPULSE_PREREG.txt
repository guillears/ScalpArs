SAMPLE / TARGETS (Step 1): every 5m boundary t from 2025-01-01 to the end of data.
  Y_btc = BTC close of the 1m bar ending t+15 / close of the 1m bar ending t - 1 (%)            (as prior study)
  Y_alt = equal-weight mean over top-50 members of close(5m bar ending t+15)/close(5m bar ending t) - 1 (%), >= 25 members.

FEATURES
 (I1) BTC spike-vs-trend, W in {15, 30} 1m bars ending at t (d_i = 1m close-to-close changes):
   er_W       efficiency ratio = (c_t - c_t-W) / sum|d_i|   (signed, -1..1)
   upsh_W     share of the W bars with d_i > 0
   spike_W    max(d_i, 0) / sum max(d_i, 0)  (share of the up-path carried by the single biggest up bar; NaN if no up bar)
   volconc_W  max 1m volume / sum volume over W   (one burst)
   volslope_W log( volume of last W/3 bars / volume of first W/3 bars )   (rising profile)
   vwap1h     c_t / VWAP(60 bars, typical price) - 1 (%)
 (I3) corr15  mean pairwise Pearson correlation of the 15 1m log-returns of the TOP-10 (members with >= 14 valid returns,
              >= 6 members, else NaN) - market-wide co-movement.
 (I2) MARKET IMPULSE SCORE = number of TRUE legs (0-9), NaN if any leg is NaN:
   L1 btc    BTC r15 > 0 AND er_15 >= q_er, q_er = median er_15 over 2025-01-01..2025-03-31 5m bars with r15 > 0 (frozen from
             features only, strictly before the first test month; logged by the feature script)
   L2 eth    ETH r15 > 0        L3 sol  SOL r15 > 0        L4 xrp  XRP r15 > 0        L5 zec  ZEC r15 > 0   (1m closes)
   L6 top10  share of TOP-10 with close(5m bar ending t)/close(5m bar ending t-15) > 1 is >= 0.70 (>= 7 valid members)
   L7 brrise top-50 trend breadth B(t) = share with close(t)/close(t-60 min) > 1 (5m closes, >= 25 members); B(t) - B(t-15) > 0
   L8 taker  TOP-10 aggregate taker-buy quote / quote volume over the last 3 closed 5m bars > 0.5
   L9 vsurge top-50 quote volume of the last 3 closed 5m bars / (top-50 quote volume of the last 288 closed 5m bars / 96) >= 1.5
   CONJ = (BTC r15 > 0) AND L6 AND L7          ("BTC up AND top-10 >= 70 % up AND breadth rising")
   SPIKE = (BTC r15 > 0) AND spike_15 >= its 2025-01..03 all-bar 80th pct AND er_15 < q_er   ("BTC rise carried by one bar")

STEP 1 — all bars, walk-forward strictly forward (test months 2025-04 .. last; train = all months strictly before)
 Scored items (23) x 2 targets = 46 tests: 11 I1 + corr15 + 9 legs (binary) + SCORE + CONJ  (SPIKE descriptive in step 1).
 Continuous features: training fixes sign (Spearman vs target) and quintile cuts; OOS spread = mean Y in top - bottom training
 quintile (bps), OOS AUC(Y>0) on the signed feature. Binary items (legs, CONJ): OOS spread = mean Y(TRUE) - mean Y(FALSE),
 sign from training. SCORE: OOS AUC of the training-oriented score; dose-response on buckets {<=2, 3, 4, 5, 6, >=7}.
 Null: target circularly shifted by a random whole number of days (7 .. N-7), 200x, whole walk-forward re-run per shift.
 STEP-1 PASS per item/target, ALL of: (a) pooled OOS AUC(Y>0) > null 99th pct (binary items: |spread| > null 99th of |spread|);
 (b) training-oriented OOS spread >= 2.0 bps in 2025 AND in 2026 test months (same orientation both years);
 (c) SCORE only: dose-response monotone — Spearman(bucket level, bucket mean Y) >= +0.8 in the oriented direction in BOTH
 years, and the >=7 vs <=2 gap >= 2 bps both years. A real effect may be continuation OR reversal; the orientation is learned
 walk-forward and must agree in both years. A Step-1 pass on EITHER target makes that item's Step-2 cells gate-eligible.
