   (pops get sold, stop-first). Predictions: P-family + (btc_ac5, btc_vr3, btc_vr12, btc_ac15, btc_ft, btc_er, alt_vr3, alt_ac5, alt_ft);
   O-family + only if regimes persist; trend/breadth (r7, dma20, br_ma20, alt_rs7) weak +; rising vol (btc_rvchg) − (transition chop);
   vol level two-sided; disp7 two-sided; voltr +; fund3 − (crowded longs); spike activity two-sided.
   PRIMARY (3, each tested alone, no family correction): btc_vr3_7 (+, NAT cut 1.0), alt_ft_7 (+, NAT cut 0), btc_ac5_3 (+, NAT cut 0).
