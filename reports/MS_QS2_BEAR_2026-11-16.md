## What QS 2 is
The slope point is always on → QS 2 = slope + ONE other point. In 9 of 11 (master + live) the other point is bear % > 65. So QS 2 = "most coins bearish, but the coin is NOT oversold (RSI not 30–35), its trend is NOT mature (ADX not 25–30), BTC NOT trending hard". A plain, early, "nothing confirms it" short.

## Bear % — the hypothesis is refuted
