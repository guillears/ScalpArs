4. Q1 RESTING TAKE-PROFIT (per sleeve)
   A(L)  = software TP: first print with net ≥ TP → market exit at the prevailing print L s later, taker; L ∈ (0.8, 1.5, 2.6).
           Stop and cap are market exits L s after their trigger in EVERY arm (same L) — Δ isolates the TP mechanism.
   B     = reduce-only post-only limit at ceil_tick(E·(TP + 100 + 0.045)/(100 − 0.018)) (net TP with a maker exit). Fills iff a print
           ≥ limit + 1 tick occurs before the stop trigger and inside the cap; booked at the limit, maker fee. Else the fill runs to its stop /
           cap (no software TP in B).
   B_touch = fills on a print ≥ limit (optimistic queue bound, context only).
   B+    = under the "through" fill rule B+ is IDENTICAL to B (a ≥ 2-tick-through print is also ≥ 1 tick through). B+ is therefore priced
           as its queue-PESSIMISTIC bound: the limit never fills; the safety net exits at market at (first print ≥ limit + 2 ticks) + 3 s + L.
   Primary comparison: B − A(L), judged at EVERY L (a candidate must pass at all three L). Reported: fill rate; A-TP fills that B misses
   (A triggers, B has not filled by A's trigger time) and their B outcome (later maker fill / stop / cap); missed-fill cost Σ(B − A) on
   them separately; B-only extra fills (B fills, A never reaches its TP).
   Parity: A at L = 0 must equal FX.evaluate on the same tick path (expect 100 %) and is compared with the replay's recorded outcome
   (same exit kind ∧ |Δ| < 0.10) → parity %.
