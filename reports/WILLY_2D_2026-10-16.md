# WILLY exhaustive 2D winners-vs-losers screen under today's exit, tick-priced fills only (2026-10-16)

Research only. No engine, config, template or test was changed. Nothing was committed and the bot API was not touched. Everything was
priced from the local cache, with no Binance calls. The pre-registration `reports/WILLY_2D_PREREG.txt` was written before any outcome
table, and the script `scripts/study_willy_2d_screen.py` asserts that it exists. This study has not been reviewed, so it supports no arm
or ship.

## Plain-English summary

**Verdict: NOTHING. No 2D (or 1D) variable separates WILLY winners from losers across the eras. There is no candidate and no observe line.**

1. **The search was exhaustive, and it found less than chance would.**
   - About 28,000 zones were tested in three families:
     - **F1:** 8,024 pair-level zones (1D plus every pair × pair combination).
     - **F2:** 15,064 pair × macro zones.
     - **F3:** 4,896 macro zones in day units.
   - Each zone had to point the same way in all three eras (2025 tick, 2026-H1, 2026-H2).
   - In every family, the best zone is **weaker than the median best zone found on shuffled outcomes**:
     - F1: 1.42 vs null median 1.91
     - F2: 1.81 vs null median 2.28
     - F3: 1.44 vs null median 1.86
   - Family-wise p is 0.94 at best. **0 survivors** at p < 0.10.
   - The count of era-consistent zones is also below the null in every family. For example, F1 has 29 zones with |S| ≥ 1, against a null
     median of 57.
2. **Walk-forward confirms nothing.**
   - Method: discover the top 20 zones in one era, then require the same sign with t ≥ 1.28 in both other eras.
   - Result: **0 confirmed in all three families**. On shuffled outcomes the same procedure confirms 1.3–1.6 by chance.
3. **The winner zones at the top of the list are luck-sized and cost money to act on.**
   - The pattern "above-VWAP share > 85 % plus something" wins 86–90 % at about +0.5–0.6 %/fill.
   - But refusing everything else is not available. A multiplier on these zones would be a W-cell, and every one of them **reverses on
     the 2025 1m-priced fills** (zone −0.4 to −0.9). Their family-wise p is ≥ 0.94.
   - Not a multiplier path.
4. **The best loser zone is the only zone that clears the expectancy bar at fill level, and it is still noise.**
   - Zone: BTC trend gap ≤ 0.024 (BTC at or under its short-term trend) while BTC is not in a deep bear (vs SMA200 > −5.5 %).
   - Tick-priced read: 153 fills on 115 days, WR 71 %, −0.37 vs +0.11. Every era is negative (−0.34 / −0.32 / −0.44).
   - Refusing it would add +0.070 %/fill, and leave-one-month-out keeps that sign in 22 of 22 months.
   - **But:**
     - It is simply the top of a 4,896-test search. Its strength is below the null median (p_fw 0.95), so a zone this good appears by
       chance more often than not.
     - On the 2025 1m-priced fills the zone is *better* than the rest (−0.37 vs −0.54), which is a refute-only reversal.
     - Master B19 refutes it: it would have refused **4 winners and 0 losers** (−4.0 pts, −$560).
     - Only the live batch supports it: it catches all 3 losers plus 2 winners. That is the same batch that generated the operator's
       question, so it counts as in-sample.
5. **The operator's live zone (vs_vwap ≤ 0.10 and run ≤ 10) does not appear in 2D either.**
   - Its nearest pre-registered quadrant (vs_vwap ≤ −0.255 and run ≤ 9.26) is direction-inconsistent: t +1.9 / −0.9 / −0.4.
   - The earlier YR6 2025 winner zone (ADX high and EMA-gap 5-8 low) is inconsistent too.
6. **What to do: leave WILLY unchanged.**
   - Today's WILLY is about +0.03 %/fill on tick-priced fills, with breakeven WR 77.3 % against a cohort WR of 78 %. That is thin, but
     no entry variable, alone or in pairs, finds the losers ahead of time.
   - Losses are spread across pairs and days; the top pair or day carries ≤ 22 % of any zone's loss.
   - Further work is better spent on the exit or the stop (the −4 stop is the whole loss tail), not on entry gates.

## Method (pre-registered, summary)

- **Variables and cuts.**
  - 54 PAIR variables and 43 MACRO variables are tested: 25 macro stamps plus the 18-variable YR6 ex-ante daily market panel.
  - Cuts are label-blind, taken from the pooled tick cohort: median, q33 / q67, and the sign split.
- **Zones.**
  - 1D: x > median, x ≤ median, x ≤ q33, x > q67, and the sign split.
  - 2D: every unordered variable pair, with 4 median quadrants and 4 outer-tercile corners.
  - Eligibility: ≥ 10 zone fills on ≥ 5 days, and ≥ 10 rest fills, in **every** era.
- **Statistic.** S = direction-consistent minimum Welch t across the 3 eras. S = 0 if any era disagrees in sign.
- **Null.** 300 shuffles of the same full search:
  - F1 / F2: day-block shuffles within each era.
  - F3: day-label permutations within each era, with the day as the unit (window-units rule).
- **Walk-forward.** Discover the top 20 in one era, then confirm with t ≥ 1.28 and the same sign in both other eras. Compared with the
  same count on the null shuffles.
- **Bar.** The expectancy filter bar, with breakeven computed on the cohort at **77.3 %**.

**Sanity checks.**
- Single-era maximum |t| sits at the null median: for example, F2 gives 9.7 / 11.2 / 8.6 against a null of 9.2 / 10.5 / 8.6.
  - These single-era maxima are inflated by small all-winner zones with zero variance. That happens equally in the null.
- The cross-era correlation of the t vectors is −0.20 to +0.10, inside the null's 5–95 % band in every case.
- So the shortfall is genuine: effects that show in one era do not carry into the others.

**Reading the F3 loser zone.**
- **Why it clears the bar but is still not a candidate.**
  - The bar items are computed on the very fills that selected the zone out of 4,896 tests.
  - The selection-aware number is p_fw = 0.95. The pre-registered rule needs p_fw < 0.05 for a candidate, or p_fw < 0.10 or a
    walk-forward confirmation for an observe line. It has neither.
  - Even taken at face value, a 30–50 % haircut leaves +0.035 to +0.049 %/fill.
  - The 2025 1m fills reverse the separation: zone −0.37 vs rest −0.54.
- **A common theme, but not new evidence.** The three F3 loser zones share one theme: BTC weak or volatile at entry. That is one
  macro family (window-units rule).
  - YR6 already found WILLY's monthly results do not track the share of falling-BTC entries (ρ −0.10).
