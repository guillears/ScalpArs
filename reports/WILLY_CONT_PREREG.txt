OPERATOR QUESTION
  "The +1.25 or +2 or the drop after 30 min is due to market conditions — we need to understand WHY a WILLY trade continues
   bullish or not."
  Q1  At the moment a WILLY fill first touches +1.25 net: what tells "continues to +2 / +3" vs "stalls / reverses"?
  Q2  For fills still open at minute 30 (the late-TP drop): what tells "recovers to +1.25" vs "bleeds"?
  Q3  At entry: is P(continuation) driven by the MARKET (BTC / ETH / SOL / breadth / market taker flow / market volume / regime of
      the day and hour) or by the COIN (its flow, volume, spike strength, turnover, cap, age)?

STEP 0 — THEORY (plain English), predictions written BEFORE testing
  A fresh pump continues when NEW buyers keep arriving after our entry: the coin's taker-buy flow stays above 50 %, its volume does
  not decay, and the market is risk-on (BTC trending up intraday, top-50 breadth up, market taker flow buying). It stalls when the
  pump's buyers are exhausted (volume decays, sellers take over the tape) or when the market turns down under it.
  Competing theory (from WILLY_THEORY 2026-10-16, losers = coin-specific dumps with BTC flat): continuation is a COIN phenomenon
  (attention / supply), and the market barely matters.
  Predicted signs (+ = more continuation / recovery):
   Q1 at the touch (closed 1m bars only, before the touch minute):
    P1  coin taker-buy share, last 5 closed 1m bars ................................. +
    P2  coin quote volume last 5 min / the 5 min before entry (volume decay) ........... +
    P3  minutes from entry to the +1.25 touch (slow = drifting, fast = impulsive) ...... − (fast continues)
    P4  worst net % before the touch (a deep dip first) ................................ + (shallower dip continues)
    P5  BTC / ETH / SOL return since the entry minute, last 5 min, last 15 min ......... +
    P6  market taker-buy share (BTC+ETH+SOL quote, last 5 closed 1m bars) .............. +
    P7  top-50 breadth (share up, last closed 5m bar; mean of last 3) and top-50 aggregate taker share (5 / 15 min) .. +
    P8  day regime (prior closed days, no look-ahead): BTC r1/r3/r7/dma20 + ; breadth br_up3/br_up7/br_ma7 + ; persistence = lag-1
        autocorrelation of BTC 15-min (and 5-min) returns over the prior 3 days + (trending tape lets runs run) ; dispersion disp7 + ;
        market volume voltr + ; prior pumps' follow-through ft2 / ft12 / ftr12 + ; realised vol rv7 / rvtr ± (no prior) ;
        intraday: BTC return since 00:00 UTC + , BTC 1m realised vol last 60 min vs prior 7 days ± , BTC+ETH+SOL quote volume last
        60 min vs prior-7-day 60-min mean + , hour of day (EU morning 08–13 UTC −, watch item from WILLY_THEORY).
    P9  coin entry stamps: spike volume multiple + ; turnover (vol/mcap) − ; mcap − ; pair age − (young coins run) ; run_pct − ;
        vs_vwap + ; wait bars − .
   Q2 at minute 30 (open fills only):
    P10 net % at minute 30 (closer to +1.25 recovers) + ; worst net % so far − (deeper = bleeds) ; best net % so far + ;
        coin taker share last 5 min + ; coin volume last 5 min / pre-entry − decay ; BTC since entry + ; breadth + ; market taker + .
   Q3 at entry: the MARKET-only model's held-out AUC vs the COIN-only model's. Pre-registered expectation: COIN ≥ MARKET (the
      competing theory); the operator's hypothesis is MARKET > COIN.
