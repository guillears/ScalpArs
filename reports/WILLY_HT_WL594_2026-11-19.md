Research only. No engine / config change. Pre-registration: `reports/WILLY_HT_WL594_2026-11-19_PREREG.md` (frozen 17:10Z, before any
variable was read against outcomes). Scripts: `scripts/willy_ht_wl594_build.py` (stamps + 1m paths + derived features),

## Question
WILLY_HT runs (paper) on +5 % net / −4 % / 24 h with FAILED_SPIKE (DL 527) and the near-spike-high dip filter (DL 594). On the 44 real
master fills left after the dip cut (24 W / 20 L, +0.86 avg, sum +38.0) — and on the 12 FAILED_SPIKE keeps (10 W / 2 L, +3.48) —
does anything else separate winners from losers? And: 44 trades at +0.86 vs 12 at +3.48 — which is better, and does dropping
FAILED_SPIKE give more trades without losing profit per day?

## Short answer
- **Nothing survives.** 90 variables / 199 1D splits: 16 nominal separators (p < 0.05) vs a shuffled-label null median of 4 and
  95th percentile 16 → P = 0.054 for the count, and the best single split (p = 0.0056) is beaten by chance in **20 %** of shuffles.
  2D (12,420 quadrant cells): best cell beaten by chance in 24 %. On the entry label WORKED: **0** nominal separators.
- The 4 strongest master splits that the backtest can price **all flip or vanish** there (DESIGN and SEEN).
- **12 > 44 on the master** (same sum, 2 stops vs 20, one-size DCR +1.56 vs +0.97 %/day, Kelly at the Wilson floor +0.27 vs −0.05).
  **On the backtest, dropping FAILED_SPIKE triples the trades and loses money per day in every period except 2025-H1**
  (ALL: 984 trades −0.31 %/day vs 299 trades −0.07 %/day; SEEN 2026-H2: −0.67 vs +0.07 %/day). More trades = more stops, not more profit.

## Data and method
- Cohort A = 44 master FRENZY_WILLY_HT fills (off_peak ≤ −1.4) re-priced on today's exit; outcome = net > 0 (all are +5.0 / −4.1).
  My own 1m re-price matched the given net on all 53 rows (49 master + 4 current). Every row matched its master stamp row (pair + opened_at).
- Cohort B = the 12 FAILED_SPIKE keeps (10 W / 2 L): no split can put ≥ 8 per side → descriptive only.
- Path labels from Binance 1m klines (entry −4 h → +24 h, cached in `reports/WILLY_HT_WL594_data/`): WORKED = +1 % before the −4 % net
  stop; REACH = best % before the stop.
- Floor per side: ≥ 8 fills and ≥ 5 distinct days. Fisher exact on WR; family-wise null = 2,000 label shuffles (+ circular-shift
  variant that keeps same-day clustering: same verdict, P = 0.069 / 0.207).
- Backtest: the rebuild episodes (`reports/WILLY_HT_THEORY_2026-11-19_data/episodes_main.pkl`) priced on today's exit with the
  theory pricer (`willy_ht_theory_eval.run_combo`, 10 slots / one per pair), dip cut applied BEFORE the portfolio. DESIGN = before
  2026-07-01, SEEN = 2026-07-01 → 11-17.

## Meaning
- At N = 44 (17 days) the winners-vs-losers question has no answer yet: the strongest master separators are exactly as strong as
  random relabelling produces, and the ones the backtest can price point the other way.
- The only recurring theme is "has the spike cooled before we buy" (FAILED_SPIKE, spike age, quieter last-15 m volume). FAILED_SPIKE is
  the one member that also holds on the backtest after July 2026 (and through the master period) — it does not hold before July.
- Extra trades from dropping FAILED_SPIKE are break-even trades with 9 of every 16 ending in a stop: on the master they add 18 stops
  for −3.8 %; on the backtest they cut the daily compound in 4 of 5 periods. **Keep FAILED_SPIKE.** More HT volume has to come from more
  qualifying signals, not from loosening this gate.

## Verdict
No new filter. No change proposed. Observe-only lines worth registering (pre-committed, frozen thresholds, read at N ≥ 30 fresh dip-cut
fills, ≥ 8 per side / ≥ 8 days): ① `pre_vol15_vs60 > 1.60` (losers) — needs the pre-entry 1m volume stamped live (not on the order
row today); ② BTC 5m EMA13 > EMA50 (losers; market-wide → window units; already refuted on the backtest, so watch only as a refute
line). Spike age is refuted by the backtest and should not be tracked.
